The EcosystemicFlâneur

Put the map down for a while. Meet what could not have been planned.

Wandering is not about getting lost for its own sake. It is a way of loosening the need to know and control everything in advance, making room for attention, encounters, discovery, and possibilities that could not have been planned.

We bring it into workshops, conferences and processes with communities, organisations and places.

Between chaos and orderNot giving up on order

Wandering does not give up on order. It moves between the two: we set out from a structure, let it loosen, and find along the way another order that could not have been planned in advance.

What wandering connectsAreas of inquiry and practice

  1. Urban wandering and attentive observation

    Walking slowly through a place, noticing what usually goes unseen: thresholds, traces of use, informal gathering spots, and the stories people attach to them.

  2. Collective intelligence and collective wisdom

    How a group that observes together can see more than any one of its members, and how individual noticing becomes shared understanding.

  3. Art of Hosting and participatory facilitation

    Hosting practices such as circle, World Café and Open Space, for conversations in which many voices can be heard.

  4. Place-making and community-led processes

    How attention to a place can become a starting point for people to shape it together, with the community at the centre.

  5. Complexity, emergence and self-organisation

    A neighbourhood as a living system, where patterns emerge from many small relationships rather than from a single plan.

For conferencesWorkshops for Conferences

These are adaptable formats rather than fixed programmes. Each can be tailored to a conference theme, venue, group size and time slot.

  1. The Urban Wandering Lab

    A guided exploration of a place through observation, curiosity and shared reflection. Small groups wander with a few open prompts, then gather to compare what they noticed.

  2. World Café: Making Space for Collective Wisdom

    A facilitated conversation designed to surface diverse perspectives and shared insights. Small tables, carefully chosen questions and several rounds that connect ideas.

  3. Wandering, Place and Community

    An experiential workshop exploring how attention to place can open new forms of participation and belonging: a short walk and a hosted conversation about the place just experienced.

  4. Collective Inquiry in Complex Times

    A participatory format for interdisciplinary dialogue, listening, and exploring questions without reducing them to simplistic binaries.

Experience and Practice

  • Facilitating dozens of wandering workshops.
  • Combining wandering with World Café at a placemaking conference in Valencia.
  • Hosting World Café conversations for large groups with Ministry of Education frameworks, leading companies, European Union programmes and many local authorities in Israel.
  • Participatory place-making projects at Dizengoff Center and in the Neve Sharet neighbourhood of Tel Aviv, Herzliya, Pardes Hanna, Ashkelon and Eilat.
  • Working with Art of Hosting practices, including circle, World Café, Open Space, storytelling, graphic facilitation and Theory U.

Ecosystemic wandering is an evolving methodological and research practice, not an established academic theory.
